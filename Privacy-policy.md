Privacy Policy — Maison Du Style

Last updated: March 13, 2026

What We Do
Maison Du Style ("we", "us") operates a Pinterest content account and associated landing pages that curate aesthetic lifestyle product recommendations. We use the Pinterest API to manage and schedule our own content.

Information We Collect
We do not collect, store, or process any personal information from Pinterest users. Our app accesses only our own Pinterest account to create and manage pins.

Pinterest API Usage
Our application uses the Pinterest API solely to:

Read our own board information
Create and manage pins on our own boards
Access our own account analytics
We do not access, collect, or store any data from other Pinterest users' accounts.

Third-Party Links
Our pins and landing pages contain affiliate links to Amazon.com. When you click these links, Amazon's own privacy policy applies. We earn a small commission on qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you.

Cookies & Tracking
We do not use cookies or tracking technologies. Third-party platforms (Pinterest, Amazon, Linktree) may use their own cookies — refer to their respective privacy policies.

Contact
For questions about this policy, reach us through our Pinterest account (@Maison_Du_Style) or Linktree page.
